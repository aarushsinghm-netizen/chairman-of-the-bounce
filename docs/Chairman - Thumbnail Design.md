# Simple chair

A square cream canvas, two lines of handwritten title, and one oversized tilted chair. The chair carries the joke without extra props or explanation.

The composition preserves clear margins and a distinct gap between title and artwork. The title stays legible at voting-card size, while the original yellow chair supplies the only bright accent.

No notebook texture, duck, coffee, desk, tagline, or additional labels. The amateur game identity comes from the borrowed chair and casual font.

The artwork remains crisp through vector rasterization. Simplicity is the organizing principle; spacing and scale do the work.
